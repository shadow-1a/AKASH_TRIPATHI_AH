Culinary Journey

Prayagraj offers an incredible street food culture that every traveler must indulge in.

Allahabadi Kachori-Sabzi

Crispy, deep-fried dal-stuffed kachoris served with a spicy, aromatic potato curry. A quintessential local breakfast staple best enjoyed around Loknath lane.

Famous Allahabadi Guava

Renowned across the nation for their exceptional quality, specifically the deep red-core variety ('Surkha') that is sweet, crisp, and fresh.
