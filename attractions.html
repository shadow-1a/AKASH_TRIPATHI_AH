Points of Interest

Triveni Sangam

The sacred confluence where the pale white Ganga meets the greenish Yamuna, offering spiritual boat rides.

Timings: 24 Hours Open (Boat rides best at Sunrise/Sunset)

Khusro Bagh

A large walled Mughal garden housing the intricate sandstone burial tombs of Prince Khusro and his family.

Timings: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Free Entry)

Chandrashekhar Azad Park

Formerly Alfred Park, this massive green space marks the historical site of freedom fighter Azad's final stand.

Timings: 5:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Nominal Fee)
