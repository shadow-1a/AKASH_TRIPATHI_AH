History & Heritage

Known anciently as Prayag ("place of sacrifice"), Prayagraj is deeply rooted in Vedic literature. According to Hindu mythology, Lord Brahma, the creator of the universe, chose this land to perform the historic Prakrishta Yajna, designating it as the king of all pilgrimage centers (Tirtharaj).

The Name Evolution

In 1583, the Mughal Emperor Akbar recognized its immense strategic and spiritual value, constructing a grand fort and naming the area Allahabad. In 2018, the state government restored its ancient identity, officially renaming the district Prayagraj.

Beyond religion, Prayagraj served as a crucible for India's intellectual and political renaissance during the British Raj. It was a nerve center for the Indian National Congress and the home base for leaders like Jawaharlal Nehru and revolutionary figures like Chandrashekhar Azad.
