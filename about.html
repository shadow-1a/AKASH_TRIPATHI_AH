About Prayagraj

The holy city of the Sangam

Prayagraj, formerly known as Allahabad, is a city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It stands at the Triveni Sangam, where the Ganga and Yamuna meet, and the Saraswati is believed to join them. The city was renamed Prayagraj in 2018.

Why it is special

  • One of Hinduism's holiest pilgrimage sites.
  • Host of the Kumbh Mela, one of the largest gatherings in the world.
  • Important in India's freedom movement, education and literature.
  • Famous for street food and Mughal-era heritage.
