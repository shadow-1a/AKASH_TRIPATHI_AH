The Maha Kumbh Mela

The Cultural Phenomenon

Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage, the Kumbh Mela occurs at Prayagraj every 12 years (Maha Kumbh) and 6 years (Ardh Kumbh). It attracts millions of pilgrims, saints, and tourists globally who unite for a holy purification dip in the Triveni Sangam.

Essential Snan (Bathing) Dates

Key celestial dates include Makar Sankranti, Paush Purnima, Mauni Amavasya (the largest crowd), and Basant Panchami.
