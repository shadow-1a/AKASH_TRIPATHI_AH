Kumbh Mela

The world's largest spiritual gathering

The Kumbh Mela is a Hindu pilgrimage in which millions of devotees bathe in sacred rivers to seek spiritual merit. It is held at four places in rotation: Prayagraj, Haridwar, Ujjain and Nashik. At Prayagraj, the bath takes place at the Triveni Sangam, where the Ganga and Yamuna meet and the Saraswati is believed to join them. UNESCO lists the Kumbh Mela as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Types of gatherings at Prayagraj

  • Magh Mela: held every year in the month of Magha (January to February).
  • Ardh Kumbh: held every 6 years.
  • Purna Kumbh: held every 12 years.
  • Maha Kumbh: held after 12 Purna Kumbhs, roughly once in 144 years.

Tips for visitors

  • Check the official dates and main bathing days before planning, and book your stay early.
  • Travel light and carry a photo ID and a charged phone.
  • Follow the instructions of officials and volunteers, especially in crowded areas.
  • Keep to the marked routes and bathing areas, and take care near deep water.
