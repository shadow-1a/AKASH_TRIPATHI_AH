Kumbh Mela Guide

The Kumbh Mela is a major Hindu pilgrimage where millions bathe at the sacred rivers. At Prayagraj it is held at the Sangam, and it rotates between four cities. Check official sources for exact dates of each Kumbh and the Magh Mela.

Tips

  • Book stay months in advance.
  • Carry ID and keep valuables safe.
  • Wear comfortable shoes for long walks.
  • Follow official crowd and bathing rules.

Getting there

  • Train: Prayagraj Junction and nearby stations.
  • Air: Prayagraj Airport (Bamrauli).
  • Road: Well connected to Varanasi, Lucknow and Kanpur.
